Exercise 7
Reading Comprehension
20 pts
Read carefully. The answers are not always written directly — sometimes you have to think.

Noa is thirteen years old. She lives in a small town where everyone knows each other. Her older brother, Avi, is a famous local singer. Noa is proud of him, but life is not always easy when your brother is famous. People stop her in the street and say: "Are you Avi's sister?" — even teachers do it. Sometimes she feels like she has no identity of her own.

Avi is planning to move to Tel Aviv next week to record his first album. He has already met a lot of new people in the music world. Noa does not understand why things have to change so fast. She knows she will miss him, but she also understands that she cannot stop him. "Things change," her mother says. "Soon you will have your own dream too."

Noa is not sure about that. But she decides to do one thing: she will find something she loves — something that is only hers — before next week. Something that has nothing to do with her famous brother.

Part A – Circle the correct answer (10 pts)

1. Why is life "not always easy" for Noa?
2. What does "even teachers do it" mean in the text?
3. Why is Avi moving to Tel Aviv?
4. What does Noa decide to do at the end?
5. What does Noa's mother mean when she says "Soon you will have your own dream too"?

Part B – Answer in full sentences (10 pts)

1. In your own words: why does Noa feel she has "no identity of her own"?
2. Find THREE words from the unit word list in the story. Write each word and the sentence it appears in.
3. Do you think Noa's feelings are understandable? Why or why not? Write at least 3 sentences.
4. What do you think Noa will choose to do? Use your imagination and write 2–3 sentences.
5. The story uses the word "change" twice. Find both uses and explain how the meaning is the same or different.
